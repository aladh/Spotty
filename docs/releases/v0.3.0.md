Spotty 0.3.0 makes it easier to pick up where you left off while browsing and keeps playback steady during account changes.

## What’s new

- Return to a recently viewed playlist, album, or artist and pick up where you left off, including your search, sort order, selection, and scroll position.
- Continue viewing playlists and albums you previously opened during a temporary connection problem. Spotty marks this saved content as possibly out of date.

## Fixes

- Keep playlists and saved music from needlessly refreshing when their details have not changed.
- Prevent delayed playback actions from affecting a new session after you sign out or reconnect.

## Improvements

- Make playback and sign-out more reliable when several changes happen at once or Spotty is closing.

## Browsing data

Spotty now saves limited playlist and album information on your Mac to support browsing continuity.
The data is private to your macOS user account but is not encrypted. Choosing **Spotty → Sign Out**
removes it. See the [local storage details](https://github.com/aladh/Spotty/blob/v0.3.0/PRIVACY.md#local-storage).

## Install

Requires **macOS 15 or newer**, an **Apple Silicon Mac**, and a **Spotify Premium account**.

From Spotty 0.2.0 or newer, choose **Spotty → Check for Updates…** to install this release.

For a manual install, download **Spotty-0.3.0.zip**, unzip it, and move **Spotty.app** to **Applications**. To verify download integrity, download **Spotty-0.3.0.zip.sha256** into the same folder and run `shasum -a 256 -c Spotty-0.3.0.zip.sha256` before opening the app.

The macOS build is **not notarized or Developer ID signed**. macOS will not automatically trust the download. If macOS blocks the first launch, dismiss the alert, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway** for Spotty. Authenticate and confirm when prompted. See the [README first-launch instructions](https://github.com/aladh/Spotty/blob/v0.3.0/README.md#first-launch-on-macos).
