Spotty 0.4.0 brings more familiar album and artist pages, with larger artwork and clearer ways to explore music.

## What's new

- Artist pages show a larger artist name. When available, they also show a banner photo, verification badge, and monthly listener count.
- Browse an artist's Popular songs, expand the list, and filter their discography by albums, singles and EPs, or compilations.
- Album pages feature larger artwork and titles, artist credits, release year, song count, and total playing time.

## Improvements

- Keep Play and the page title within reach as you scroll album and artist pages.
- Return to an artist page without losing your scroll position, track selection, expanded sections, or release filter.

## Track table changes

- The BPM, musical key, and Popularity columns and their sorting options have been removed from track tables.

## Install

Requires **macOS 15 or newer**, an **Apple Silicon Mac**, and a **Spotify Premium account**.

From Spotty 0.2.0 or newer, choose **Spotty → Check for Updates…** to install this release.

For a manual install, download **Spotty-0.4.0.zip**, unzip it, and move **Spotty.app** to **Applications**. To verify download integrity, download **Spotty-0.4.0.zip.sha256** into the same folder and run `shasum -a 256 -c Spotty-0.4.0.zip.sha256` before opening the app.

The macOS build is **not notarized or Developer ID signed**. macOS will not automatically trust the download. If macOS blocks the first launch, dismiss the alert, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway** for Spotty. Authenticate and confirm when prompted. See the [README first-launch instructions](https://github.com/aladh/Spotty/blob/v0.4.0/README.md#first-launch-on-macos).
