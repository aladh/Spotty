Spotty 0.5.2 makes artist browsing smoother and adds more ways to explore the music you’re hearing.

## Improvements

- Read artist biographies in the new **About** section, with photos and available audience counts.
- Browse **Discovered on** and **Artist Playlists** below About when Spotify provides them.
- Open the current track’s album by clicking its title or artwork, or open an artist by clicking their name.

## Fixes

- Scroll artist pages vertically while the pointer is over Discography or playlist rows.
- Show a pointing hand over cards and current-track links, with underlined text links on hover.

## Install

Requires **macOS 15 or newer**, an **Apple Silicon Mac**, and a **Spotify Premium account**.

From Spotty 0.2.0 or newer, choose **Spotty → Check for Updates…** to install this release.

For a manual install, download **Spotty-0.5.2.zip**, unzip it, and move **Spotty.app** to **Applications**. To verify download integrity, download **Spotty-0.5.2.zip.sha256** into the same folder and run `shasum -a 256 -c Spotty-0.5.2.zip.sha256` before opening the app.

The macOS build is **not notarized or Developer ID signed**. macOS will not automatically trust the download. If macOS blocks the first launch, dismiss the alert, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway** for Spotty. Authenticate and confirm when prompted. See the [README first-launch instructions](https://github.com/aladh/Spotty/blob/v0.5.2/README.md#first-launch-on-macos).
