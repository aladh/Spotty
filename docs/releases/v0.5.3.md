Spotty 0.5.3 makes startup and browsing smoother, with saved content appearing sooner and better keyboard access throughout your library.

## Improvements

- Show your saved playlist library while Spotify refreshes it, once your account is verified. Previously loaded playlists and albums also show saved content while refreshing.
- Keep Search results, selected songs and scroll position visible when retrying the same search. Retry failed album and artist refreshes with **Try Again** while keeping the music already on screen.
- Use the keyboard to reach artwork playback controls in the library, Queue, Recently played and Search previews. Library folders also support keyboard expansion.
- Keep Home and its recommendation rows at your previous scroll position when you return. Wide windows show a consistent four-column shortcut layout, and recommendation cards include playlist descriptions.

## Fixes

- Pause and resume the current selection from Search preview and Discography controls without restarting the track. Playlist shuffle stays with the selected playlist.
- Include newly observed listening and resumes on other Spotify devices in Recently played, with accurate recorded times and links to known artists.
- Preserve current-track album and artist links as more information arrives, keep repeated recommendation cards visible, and wrap long Discography titles more reliably.
- Keep the library steadier during navigation and reconnects, with clearer empty and failed-load messages. When Spotify rejects a Search or artist request, show the sign-in error, including under Search category filters, instead of keeping old results or spinning indefinitely.

## Install

Requires **macOS 15 or newer**, an **Apple Silicon Mac**, and a **Spotify Premium account**.

From Spotty 0.2.0 or newer, choose **Spotty → Check for Updates…** to install this release.

For a manual install, download **Spotty-0.5.3.zip**, unzip it, and move **Spotty.app** to **Applications**. To verify download integrity, download **Spotty-0.5.3.zip.sha256** into the same folder and run `shasum -a 256 -c Spotty-0.5.3.zip.sha256` before opening the app.

The macOS build is **not notarized or Developer ID signed**. macOS will not automatically trust the download. If macOS blocks the first launch, dismiss the alert, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway** for Spotty. Authenticate and confirm when prompted. See the [README first-launch instructions](https://github.com/aladh/Spotty/blob/v0.5.3/README.md#first-launch-on-macos).
